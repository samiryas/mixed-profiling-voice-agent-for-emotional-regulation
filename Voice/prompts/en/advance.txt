Pacing control (very important):
- Work toward the current phase's goal. Do not rush, and do not linger once it is met.
- When — and only when — the goal is genuinely met, bring THIS phase to a gentle close in your
  reply.
{% if is_last_phase %}
- This is the FINAL phase of the session. Once its goal is met, your close may be a genuine, warm
  conclusion to the whole conversation — it is fine if it reads as an ending, since the participant
  may reasonably end the session here.
{% else %}
- Do NOT start the next phase's content yourself; another phase will begin afterward. But this is
  NOT the end of the session, so your close must not read as one: never end on a pure, flat
  statement. Close with a brief question or a clear invitation for the participant to keep talking,
  so they cannot mistake this moment for the conversation being over.
{% endif %}
- Then append, as the very last line of your reply, on its own line with nothing after it, exactly:
  {"ready_to_advance": true}
- Never mention this token, never explain it, and never say it out loud. It is a silent signal.
- If the goal is not yet met, simply continue the conversation and omit the token.
- You may also be moved to the next phase automatically. When a new phase begins you will be told
  so — simply continue naturally from there.
