Dear participant,
thank you for participating in our experiment and in this survey conducted by the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), Germany.

This is the second part of a two-part study.

  • Part 1 (14th of January): survey with 55 questions about demographics, personality, and wellbeing (~ 10 minutes)
  • Part 2 (TODAY): survey with 31 questions + chatbot interaction (~ 30 minutes)
  • Total time commitment: ~ 45 minutes across both parts

The overall goal of this study is to evaluate a process for creating user profiles for chatbot interactions. Your participation will help us develop more personalized and effective AI interactions that better meet users' needs.

WHAT HAPPENS IN THIS PART OF THE STUDY?
This second part of the study has the following structure:

  • Section 1: You will be asked several questions regarding your personality and mental health.
  • Section 2: You will interact with a chatbot and answer open questions regarding your personality and mental health.
  • Section 3: You will evaluate different dimensions of your created profile.
  • Section 4: You will refine your profile.
In total, this study part takes around 30 minutes. Please provide thoughtful and honest responses to all questions.
